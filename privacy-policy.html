Privacy Policy

At Market Koro, we take your personal privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, store, use, and protect your information when you shop with us.

Information We Collect

  • Account Info: Your name, email address, and mobile number provided during sign-up or checkout.
  • Delivery Address: Division, district, upazila, union, and village details for delivering orders in Kushtia and across Bangladesh.
  • Payment Details: Transaction IDs for bKash payments. We do not store PINs or passwords.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for order processing, delivery updates, invoice generation, and customer support. We never sell or share your personal information with unauthorized third parties.
