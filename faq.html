FAQ & Help Center
Ordering & Delivery
How do I place an order on Market Koro?
Browse our catalog or search for products, click "Add to Cart", proceed to checkout, enter your shipping details, select your payment method (Cash on Delivery or bKash), and click "Place Order".
What are the delivery charges?
Standard home delivery inside Kushtia town is only ৳100 (1-2 business days). For deliveries outside Kushtia across Bangladesh, the charge is ৳160 (3-5 business days).
Can I track my order status?
Yes! You can view live updates on your order status (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered) in the My Orders page or inside your account profile.
Payment Options
What payment methods are supported?
We support Cash on Delivery (COD) for convenient payment upon receipt, as well as bKash Send Money / Payment with instant Transaction ID verification.
How do I pay using bKash?
During checkout, choose bKash, send the total amount to our official bKash number 01342697743, and enter your 10-character TrxID in the popup modal to complete your order.
Returns & Refunds
What is your return policy?
We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy if you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product. Contact our support team with your Order ID and photo proof to initiate a replacement or refund.
